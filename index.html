Our Purpose

Defense Is Subjective.
Make That Your Advantage.

Was that close-out good enough? Did the contest actually alter the shot? Did your corner defender execute the right read, even though the basket still fell? These are the judgment calls your staff debates every film session, and they're invisible to traditional analytics. Coach's Analytics captures them all: close-out grades, contest quality, deflections, and credit for good defense on a made basket.

It also identifies when an opponent's offense is exposing your system and surfaces concrete scheme adjustments before the damage compounds. Every possession is tracked at the action level so your staff can walk into the locker room and deliver exactly this:
Here is exactly what broke down. Here is who needs to fix it. Here is the one sentence that will fix it.

For NBA Organizations

The Framework Is Already There.
Your Cameras Make It Unstoppable.

NBA teams already have Second Spectrum, SportVU, and machine-vision tools that track every movement on the floor. What those systems cannot capture is the subjective layer: whether the close-out was right, whether the contest was well-timed, whether a defender executed the correct rotation on a basket that still fell. That layer is invisible to cameras and absent from every current analytics platform.

Coach's Analytics provides exactly that layer. Paired with existing NBA tracking infrastructure, it creates something no team currently has: objective spatial data married to real-time subjective grading, unified into a single actionable read on every possession. The result is a complete defensive picture, and a discernible competitive edge, that is available right now.

Close-Out Grades

Grade every close-out, every possession

After each shot, defenders are graded 1–5 on their close-out execution. These grades accumulate into the most specific defensive metric in the game: not whether a shot went in, but how well the defense contested it.

1
No Contest
No effort on close-out. Shooter had open look.
2
Late
Arrived after shot. Shooter unaffected.
3
Adequate
Contested but late or flat-footed.
4
Strong
Timely, contested, recovered well.
5
Elite
Hand up early, forced a miss or foul.
